## How to pay

Customers can pay cash on delivery, or online through SSLCOMMERZ, which offers bKash, cards and
other mobile banking on its own secure page. Payment details go straight to the payment provider:
the store never sees or stores card numbers.

## When an online payment is confirmed

An order paid online is confirmed when the payment provider confirms the payment, not when the
browser returns to the store. The order page shows the payment's current state and updates by
itself.
