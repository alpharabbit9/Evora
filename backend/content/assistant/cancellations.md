## Cancelling an order

A customer can cancel an order themselves from its order page until the order starts being
prepared. After that it can no longer be cancelled online. If the order was already paid online,
the payment is refunded to the way it was paid. The assistant cannot cancel orders; the Cancel
button is on the order page.
