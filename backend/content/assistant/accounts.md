## Signing in

Adding to the bag, saving to the wishlist and checking out need an account. Creating one is free
from the sign-in page. A forgotten password can be reset from the sign-in page.

## Orders

Signed-in customers see their orders, with progress and tracking, on their account page and on
each order's page.
