## Where we deliver

We deliver within Bangladesh only. International shipping is not offered yet; the countries we
serve will be listed at checkout once that is decided.

## Delivery zones

There are two delivery zones. Orders to an address in Dhaka are charged the Dhaka rate, and all
other addresses in Bangladesh are charged the rate for elsewhere. The fee for an order is shown at
checkout, before paying, and is worked out from the delivery address.

## Delivery options and times

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery, when the store offers it,
usually takes 1 to 2 working days. Estimates are not guarantees. Orders over a threshold the store
sets may be delivered free; checkout shows whether an order qualifies.

## Tax

Any tax is added at checkout and shown before paying.
