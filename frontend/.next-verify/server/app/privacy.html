Privacy

What this store records about you, and what is still to be decided.

Draft

This page is a draft summary, not a legal privacy policy. It has not been legally reviewed, and how long data is kept and how it can be deleted are not decided yet.

What we record

Your account: your name, your email address and your password, and the sign-in sessions that keep you signed in.

Your orders: the items bought and their prices at the time, the delivery address and phone number you enter at checkout, the delivery option, and the status of the order and its payment. The email address on an order is your account's.

Your bag and wishlist: the pieces you add to them, while you are signed in.

Payments

Online payments are taken on the payment provider's own page (SSLCOMMERZ, which includes bKash, cards and other mobile banking). Your payment details go straight to the provider: we never see or store them. We keep the outcome of the payment and the provider's reference for it. Cash on delivery involves no online payment.

The online payment connection is currently a test (sandbox) one.

Still to be decided

How long order, payment and account records are kept, how accounts can be deleted, who can see this data, where it is hosted and how email is sent are not decided yet. Until they are, this page makes no promise about them.

Draft

A reviewed privacy policy is required before launch (PRD §8, §9).
